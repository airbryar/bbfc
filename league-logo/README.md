# League logo

Upload the BBFC logo here and the site uses it in three places:

- the top of the site
- the browser tab
- the home-screen icon when someone adds the site to their phone

**To add or replace it**

1. Tap **Add file**, then **Upload files**
2. Add your picture named **logo.png** (a PNG with a see-through background looks best)
3. Tap **Commit changes**

The site updates within a couple of minutes. To replace it, upload a new
**logo.png**. To go back to the built-in BBFC seal, delete logo.png from this folder.

Phones that already saved the site to their home screen keep the old icon until
the person removes it and adds the site again.
